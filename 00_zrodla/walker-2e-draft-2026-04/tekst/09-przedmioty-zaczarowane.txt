Enchanted Objects
“‘I solemnly swear that I am up 
to no good,’ he said. A thin, ink 
line traced itself across the map, 
then, like a spider’s web, more 
lines appeared, until a very 
detailed map of Hogwarts spread 
out in front of him.” 
Enchanted or bewitched 
objects are the closest 
equivalent wizarding kind has 
to Muggle technology. These 
are a good starting point to see 
how enchanted objects are 
created and will hopefully 
inspire you to create some 
weird and wonderful devices. 
They don’t cover some unique 
items, like the deluminator 
and other inventions of 
Dumbledore, but they give less 
legendary witches and wizards 
plenty to play with. 
Creating enchanted objects 
requires a combination of 
Transfiguration, Charms, and 
if it’s not an existing object, 
the Crafting skill, but first you 
must decide what magical 
qualities your object will 
possess. 
The table to the top–right
lists all of the qualities an
enchanted object can 
possess. Each quality has 
an associated difficulty. 
The difficulty to enchant the
item will be 10 plus the
combined difficulty of all the
qualities imbued. 
You can see examples of 
enchanted objects on pages 
74–76 along with the qualities 
they are imbued with and the 
total difficulty to create each 
item. 
Example 
Let’s say you want to recreate 
the Marauder’s Map. The map 
shows every inch of Hogwarts 
(Broadcasting), inside and out, 
including secret passages 
(Penetrating), and the location 
of every person in the 
grounds. The map can 
accurately identifying each 
person, and is not fooled by 
Animagi, Polyjuice Potions, or 
Invisibility Cloaks. Even the 
Hogwarts ghosts are not 
exempt (Standard spell:
Homonculous Charm). 
The map hid its contents 
(Disguising) and only revealed 
itself when it heard the 
command "I solemnly swear 
that I am up to no good” and 
reacted to the presence of 
Severus Snape by displaying 
insults (intelligent). 
 
In summary, 10+ 
Broadcasting (3) 
Penetrating (5) 
Standard spell (5) 
Disguising (7) 
Intelligent (5) 
Total difficulty: 35. 
A hefty target worthy of four 
very talented wizards.

Qualities 
Accelerated 
Adds extra speed dice to a 
vehicle or animated object. 
An animated enchanted 
object, like a broomstick, has a 
base speed of 1D and the 
speed of various motorised 
Muggle vehicles are listed 
below. Every+1D of additional 
speed adds 5 points of 
difficulty. 
Animated 
Moves independently at 1D 
speed. Can hover or move 
through the air at ground 
level, but cannot fly at altitude 
without the Flying Charm. 
Can be dangerously random 
without the Intelligent or 
Loyal qualities. 
Bestowing 
Transfers the enchanted item’s 
power to the wearer or holder 
For example, without the 
Bestowing quality, an 
invisibility clock would be 
transparent and the person 
within it perfectly visible. 
Broadcasting 
Visually or audibly delivers 
specific information. 
Scoreboards at the Quidditch 
World Cup and wizarding 
wireless radios both employ 
this quality. 
Detecting 
Locates defined targets and 
can activate other qualities or 
raise the alarm based on 
specific stimuli. The alarm can 
be audible, visual, or both. 
Disguising 
Alters or distorts the 
appearance of the enchanted 
object. Requires the 
Bestowing quality to disguise 
the user of the enchanted 
object. A hidden door might 
use this quality to resemble 
the brickwork around it and 
then use the Detecting quality 
to hear a password and then 
reveal itself. 
Dispensing 
Delivers a specific item or 
substance. Can be dispensed 
at a gentle pace or fired at 
high speed when paired with 
the Accelerated quality. 
Enhanced spell 
Embodies an enhanced 
version of a spell effect. 
Enhancements are at the 
discretion of the Narrator and 
should ideally add character 
and functionality to an 
enchanted item rather than 
being used to power play. 
Each enhanced spell add +10
difficulty. 
Intelligent 
Knows information in a 
specific area, can make simple 
decisions, and can respond to 
instructions. 
Loyal 
Paired to an individual and 
always works to their 
advantage. Can pair with a 
new owner over time. 
Penetrating 
Can perceive through walls 
and other barriers. 
Slippery 
Moves seamlessly through 
gaps smaller than itself and 
evades hazards in its path like 
the Knight Bus. 
Standard spell 
Embodies a standard spell 
effect. Each standard spell 
effect adds +5 difficulty. 
Time twisting 
Can change the flow of time. 
Returning one or two 
individuals to an earlier point 
in time, or like the Bell Jar of 
Time (see page 74), warping 
time in a limited area. Messing 
with time is highly dangerous, 
and both players and narrators 
should tread warily.

Enchanted Objects
Crafting 
You can enchant an existing 
object, but crafting it yourself 
gives you a relationship with 
the object that will make 
enchanting easier. Roll 
1D+Knowledge+Crafting for 
your Crafting Score. 
You will also need to make a 
Transfiguration roll to prime 
your object for enchantment: 
1D+Focus+Transfiguration. 
The target for your 
Transfiguration roll will 
depend on your Crafting 
Score (see below), or if you are 
enchanting an existing object. 
Up to three other witches or 
wizards can assist you with the 
crafting and transfiguration. 
Add +1 to your roll for each 
helper. 
If the item is not of the quality 
you were hoping for, you will 
have to start again. If your 
Transfiguration roll fails, the 
item is destroyed and you will 
also have to start from scratch. 
Enchanting 
Roll 1D+Focus+Charms. 
Your target is the difficulty of 
the enchanted item based on 
the qualities you want to 
bestow (see page 71). 
You can add a bonus for any 
assistance, as shown on the 
table (bottom–right). 
Research 
For every six hours you spend 
in the library and roll 12+ with 
1D10+Knowledge+Archivist
you gain the cumulative bonus 
below. You can split this time 
over multiple day. 
 
Helping Hands 
Up to three others can assist 
with the enchanting, as per 
the normal rules for working 
together (see page 4), each 
helper adds +1 to your roll. 
Perseverance 
If you fail to enchant the item, 
you can spend more time 
tinkering with it and try a 
week later with a +1 bonus. 
The Misuse of Muggle 
Artefacts Office is a division of 
the Department of Magical 
Law Enforcement, based on 
the second floor of the British 
Ministry of Magic. 
The Department regulates the 
use of magic on Muggle 
objects and tries to keep items 
that have been bewitched 
away from Muggles. 
The law prohibits the 
enchantment of Muggle items 
with the intent to use them for 
purposes other than they were 
designed for (Arthur Weasley 
claimed he was able to 
bewitch a Ford Anglia to fly 
legally because he did not 
intend to fly the car). Beware 
when enchanting objects that 
are common in the Muggle 
world that they don’t fall into 
Muggle hands.

Broomstick 
Broomsticks, also known as 
brooms, are a means of 
transportation employed by 
wizards and witches. Nearly 
every wizarding household in 
Britain owns at least one 
broomstick. Young wizards 
begin their broomstick 
practice with non–accelerated 
broomsticks. Very speedy 
sports brooms are used for 
games like Quidditch. 
Qualities: Accelerated (not 
included for training brooms), 
Animated, Loyal, Standard 
spell (Flying Charm and the 
Molliare cushioning charm to 
make the broom more 
comfortable for the rider) 
Difficulty: 26 (+5 per extra 1D 
of Acceleration) 
Dumbledore's Army coin 
Fake Galleons created by 
Hermione Granger, in order to 
call meetings of Dumbledore's 
Army, a secret group of 
students dedicated to teaching 
fellow Hogwarts students 
Defence Against the Dark 
Arts. The numbers around the 
edge of the coin, normally just 
a serial number identifying the 
goblin who cast the coin, 
changed to indicate when the 
next meeting would take place. 
The coins came in handy 
during the Second Wizarding 
War, and after the war were 
kept as badges of honour. 
Qualities: Standard spell 
(Protean charm). 
Difficulty: 15
 
Durmstrang bow staff
