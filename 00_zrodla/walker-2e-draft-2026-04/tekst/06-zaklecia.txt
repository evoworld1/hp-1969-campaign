Difficulty: 12

landing may cause 2D or 3D

Narrator’s discretion.

Protego 
Type: Charm 
Difficulty: 15
The Shield Charm. Protects 
the caster with an invisible 
barrier that blocks all physical 
attacks and all but the most 
powerful jinxes, hexes, and 
curses (it does nothing to stop 
the Unforgivable Curses). 
Damage rolled for any attack 
that hits the barrier is reduced 
by 30 points. If multiple spells 
hit you at the same time, the 
protection is shared. e.g. three 
simultaneous blasts will be 
reduced by 10 points each. 
Protego Horribilis 
Type: Charm 
Needs: Protego Totalum 
Difficulty: 21
The most powerful version of 
the Shield Charm. As with 
Protego Totalum, it protects an 
area but is much stronger. 
Damage rolled for any attack 
that hits the barrier is reduced 
by 90 points. If multiple spells 
hit you at the same time, the 
protection is shared. e.g. three 
simultaneous blasts will be 
reduced by 30 points each. 
Protego Totalum 
Type: Charm 
Needs: Protego 
Difficulty: 18
A version of the shield charm 
that protects an area. Damage 
rolled for any attack that hits 
the barrier is reduced by 60
points. If multiple spells hit 
you at the same time, the 
protection is shared. e.g. three 
simultaneous blasts will be 
reduced by 20 points each.

Difficulty: 12

Stupefy 
Type: Charm 
Difficulty: 15
The Stunning Spell. Also 
known as the Stupefying 
Charm, or Stunner for short. 
Much favoured for Duelling as 
it renders the victim 
unconscious without lasting 
damage, although multiple 
stuns can be injurious and 
even deadly. When you are hit 
by a stunner, roll 1D10+ 
Vitality. You need to roll 12+ to 
stay conscious. When multiple 
Stunners are fired at the same 
time, the target rises by +2 for 
each additional Stunner. In 
addition the victim has to roll 
to resist physical damage 
equal to one dice per 
additional Stunner (rolled as 
per the rules for resisting 
physical damage on page 35). 
For example, if you are hit by 
three Stunners, you need to 
roll 16+ to stay conscious and 
will have to resist 2D physical 
damage. The counter-Charm 
for Stupefy is Rennervate, the 
Reviving Charm (see page 57).
