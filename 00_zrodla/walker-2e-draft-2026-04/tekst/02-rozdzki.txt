Bonuses to particular skills, 
like Divination or Herbology, 
and particular types of magic, 
like Charms or Hexes, are easy 
to see where they apply, but 
which spells count as Fire 
Magic or Water Magic? 
This page specifies which 
spells are included in each 
category. 
If bonuses overlap, like a 
bonus to all Charms paired 
with a bonus to a specific 
Charm, then both bonuses 
apply.
