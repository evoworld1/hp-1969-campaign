Potions
“I can teach you how to bewitch 
the mind and ensnare the senses. 
I can tell you how to bottle fame, 
brew glory, and even put a 
stopper in death.” 
Studying potions is 
treated the same way as 
learning spells (see page 
40). Learning a particular 
potion is represented by filling 
in the boxes next to the name 
of the potion on your magic 
sheet. For example: 
Each box contains a number. 
The number in the leftmost 
box is the current difficulty. 
Once you’ve rolled the current 
difficulty to mix a dose of that 
potion you can fill in the 
current box and the next box 
along becomes the new target. 
Once all the boxes are filled in 
you’ve mastered that potion 
and you no longer need to roll 
to mix it. 
Creating a potion is split into 
three stages: 
Stage 1. Gathering. 
This is where you collect your 
ingredients (see the potion list 
on pages 65–66). Some 
common items, like dried 
Nettles or Castor Oil, may be 
in your store–cupboard 
already (or the shared student 
store–cupboard). Others will 
need to be purchased from an 
Apothecary like Slug and 
Jigger’s in Diagon Alley, but 
beware. Some rarer items may 
be on the Restricted Register 
for Potion Ingredients and 
require a little more 
subterfuge to acquire. 
Gathering all the ingredients 
for a powerful potion can be a 
whole adventure in itself. 
Common ingredients can be 
foraged or harvested but even 
that will present its own 
challenges. As anybody who is 
ever tried to pluck a 
Jobberknoll can tell you. 
Finding plants in the wild will 
at some stage involve rolling 
1D+Knowledge+Herbology
(see page 67), while hands on 
activities (like milking a 
Bubotuber for its pus) will 
require a strong stomach and 
rolling 1D+Wits+Magical
Creatures. 
Stage 2. Mixing. 
This is where the ingredients 
are prepared and combined. 
Do you chop or squash, stir 
clockwise or anti–clockwise or 
both, do you simmer gently or 
boil furiously. Every detail will 
effect the outcome. 
You’ll also need some working 
space. The Potions Classroom 
at Hogwart’s offers all the 
standard lab facilities, but 
sometimes you’ll need to 
rough it in a rudimentary lab
(hopefully not as rudimentary 
as a toilet stall in the second–
floor girls’ lavatory). You may 
even get to work in a luxurious 
lab like Nicolas Flamel’s in 
Paris. Unlike Flamel, you may 
not have the gift of time and 
it’s possible to shave up to a 
quarter off the time required 
by cutting corners with a 
slapdash effort. However 
painstaking effort will generally 
yield the best results as will 
using an authoritative reference 
work like Most Potente 
Potions. An old text book with 
annotations by a gifted 
potioneer could also be 
equally valuable. Having the 
help of a lab assistant will 
always be beneficial, but better 
to have access to expert 
assistance like a Potions Master. 
If you’ve mastered the potion 
you will be able to brew it 
without rolling. Otherwise 
you’ll need to roll 
1D+Knowledge+Potions and 
compare it to the difficulty to 
reveal the quality of your 
potion (see page 49). 
You target will be the current 
difficulty of the potion on your 
magic sheet +/- the following 
modifiers:

Stage 3. Bottling. 
Until you become an 
accomplished Potioneer, you 
can never be completely sure 
of your success until the 
potion is imbibed. 
When you finish mixing your 
potion you will need to roll 
1D+Knowledge+Potions and 
compare the result to the 
current difficulty of the potion 
(+/- the modifiers on page 60). 
This will reveal the potion’s 
apparent Quality: 
If you’ve mastered the potion 
(filled in all the numbered 
boxes next to the potion on 
your magic sheet) you don’t 
need to roll and your potion 
automatically has a quality of 
+3. The Narrator may decide 
to drop that to +2 if you were 
working under enormous time 
pressure or with very basic 
equipment. 
The Narrator secretly rolls 
1D10 +/– your quality score 
and consults the table below. 
The Narrator can also add or 
subtract one or two points in 
the event of special 
circumstances. 
The results can be poisonous 
(Narrator’s discretion), have 
side–effects (lasting 1D5 hours 
or until an antidote can be 
administered), have no effect, 
or the effect expected. They 
can also be highly effective 
(see potion descriptions). 
Ageing Potion 
Difficulty: 15
Temporarily ages the 
recipient. The more that they 
drink, the older they get. A 
highly effective Ageing Potion 
makes you ancient in mere 
seconds. 
Amortentia 
Difficulty: 15
The World’s strongest love 
potion. The drinker becomes 
infatuated with the person 
who gave it to them. Love 
potions wear off after 24 hours 
unless another dose (or the 
antidote) is given. The effects 
of highly effective Amortentia 
can last up to 48 hours and 
only a highly effective version 
of the antidote is effective 
against it. 
Animagus Potion 
Difficulty: 18
Part of the complex Animagus 
ritual that bestows the ability 
to transform into an animal. 
For details of the ritual see 
page 43. You will also need to 
have mastered the Animagus 
spell on page 47. A highly 
effective draught has an 
additional calming effect that 
entirely avoids the panicked 
destruction that can 
sometimes accompany the 
transformation. 
Babbling Beverage 
Difficulty: 9
Causes the drinker to babble 
and speak nonsense. Effect 
lasts for half an hour, highly 
effective version twice that.

Potions
Befuddlement 
Draught 
Difficulty: 9
Temporarily causes the 
drinker to become belligerent 
and reckless. While affected 
they argue with everybody and 
dangerously overestimate their 
abilities. The effects lasts for 
half an hour, highly effective 
versions last for twice that. 
Blood Replenishing 
Potion 
Difficulty: 12
Replenishes blood loss. 
Reduces body injuries when 
bloodied or above by one 
circle (bloodied becomes 
bruised, for example). A highly 
effective version reduces 
injury by two circles. 
Burn Healing Paste 
Difficulty: 12
A thick orange paste used to 
treat burns. Applied daily, it 
can heal even severe burns 
within a month or two. Highly 
effective burn paste can do the 
same within a week or two. 
Calming Draught 
Difficulty: 15
Enforces a feeling of calm and 
serenity after suffering a 
terrible shock or emotional 
episode. Reduces your mental 
state by one circle (raving 
becomes confused, for 
example) while a highly 
effective Draught reduces it by 
two circles. 
Confusing 
Concoction 
Difficulty: 9
Temporarily causes the 
drinker to become confused 
and incoherent. Normally 
while under a Confusing 
Concoction’s effects, the 
victim’s mental state becomes 
confused (+5 penalty added to 
any target they need to roll), 
but a highly effective 
concoction causes raving (+7
penalty). The effects lasts for 
half an hour, highly effective 
versions last for twice that. 
Cure for Boils 
Difficulty: 9
Cures boils, pustules, 
and hives, including those 
produced by the Pimple Jinx 
(Furnunculus). As well as 
clearing the skin of boils, a 
highly effective dose leaves the 
skin noticeably healthy and 
flawless for up to 24 hours. 
Deflating Draught 
Difficulty: 9
Causes anything swollen by 
magic (the Engorgement 
Charm, for example) to return 
to normal size and acts as an 
antidote to the Swelling 
Solution. The swelling can 
take up to an hour to fully 
shrink back to normal, but a 
highly effective draught can 
achieve the same result in 
seconds. 
Draught of 
Living Death 
Difficulty: 18
An extremely powerful 
sleeping draught. It sends the 
drinker into a deathlike 
slumber similar to suspended 
animation. The effects can be 
cancelled with the Mandrake 
Restorative Draught. However, 
a highly effective draught will 
require a highly effective 
Mandrake Restorative Draught 
to counter it. 
Draught of Peace 
Difficulty: 15
Calms anxiety and soothes 
agitation, but made incorrectly 
it can put the drinker into a 
deep and possibly irreversible 
sleep (alternative to poisonous 
result for potion effect). 
Reduces your mental state by 
one circle (raving becomes 
confused, for example). A 
highly effective draught also 
boosts your mental focus, 
adding +2 to any rolls you 
make for an hour. 
Elixar to Induce 
Euphoria 
Difficulty: 15
A sunshine–yellow potion that 
makes the drinker irrationally 
joyful and prone to singing, 
nose tweaking, and other 
irritatingly cheerful behaviour. 
The highly effective version 
brings joy with a pleasing hint 
of Peppermint and without the 
irritating side–effects.

Potions
Felix Felicis 
(Liquid Luck) 
Difficulty: 18
A potion with the 
appearance of molten 
gold that makes the 
drinker unbelievably 
lucky for a period of 
time during which anything 
they attempt will be 
successful. Taking repeated 
doses is unwise as it causes 
giddiness, recklessness, and 
dangerous overconfidence. 
Large quantities can even be 
deadly. A single dose lasts for 
one hour, a highly effective 
dose last for three hours. 
Fire Protection 
Potion 
Difficulty: 9
The Fire Protection Potion, 
also known as the Ice Potion, 
is a potion used to protect the 
drinker from flames. It 
induces a sensation of ice and 
frigidity and provides 
protection from most magical 
fires. A highly effective Fire 
Protection potion makes one 
immune to all magical flames, 
including FiendFyre flames. 
Forgetfulness Potion 
Difficulty: 9
Causes the recipient difficulty 
in recalling information or 
forming memories for one 
hour. Following that hour they 
will only have vague memories 
of what happened during that 
time. A highly effective 
Forgetfulness Potion will leave 
them with no memory of what 
happened. 
Hair Raising Potion 
Difficulty: 12
Causes your hair to stand on 
end. The highly effective 
version grows your hair at the 
same time so even short hair 
ends up waving high above 
your head like a tall hat. 
Invigoration Draught 
Difficulty: 12
Removes all fatigue and makes 
you feel like you’ve just woken 
from a great night’s sleep. Your 
energy reserves will remain 
boundless for up to eight 
hours or twelve with a highly 
effective draught. 
Love Potion 
Antidote 
Difficulty: 15
Immediately halts the effects 
of a Love Potion. A highly 
effective Love Potion Antidote 
is required to combat a highly 
effective Love Potion. 
Mandrake 
Restorative Draught 
Difficulty: 12
The Mandrake Draught can 
return people who have been 
transfigured or cursed to their 
original state and is the 
standard cure for Petrification. 
Particularly extensive 
transfigurations and very 
powerful curses (the Imperius 
curse, for example) may 
require a highly effective 
Mandrake Restorative Draught 
to reverse. 
Murtlap Essence 
Difficulty: 9
Soothes and heals painful cuts 
and abrasions. Even deep cuts 
will heal completely after a 
day or two. Highly effective 
Murtlap Essence delivers the 
same effect within an hour or 
two of being applied. 
Pepperup Potion 
Difficulty: 9
Used to warm up people who 
are cold and miserable. It is 
also a cure for the common 
cold. A highly effective dose 
can also cure a range of other 
minor ailments including 
Influenza, earache, and 
Athletes Foot 
Polyjuice Potion 
Difficulty: 15
Allows the drinker to assume 
the form of another person. 
They can be a different sex or 
age, but must be human. 
A highly complicated, 
challenging, and time￾consuming potion that takes at 
least two months to complete. 
The potion requires a part of 
the living person who is being 
imitated. A hair, a toenail 
clipping, dandruff, or worse. 
The effects of the potion last 
for 1D5 hours (rolled secretly 
by the Narrator). A highly 
effective Polyjuice Potion is 
much more reliable and lasts 
for 2D5+2 hours. The Narrator 
may of course override the 
rolled time if the reversal of 
the transformation at an 
earlier or later point is more 
dramatic or amusing.

Shrinking Solution 
Difficulty: 15
Causes creatures to shrink to a 
younger form. A standard 
Shrinking Solution will 
reduce a Toad to a Tadpole, or 
a Sheep to a Lamb. A highly 
effective version will also make 
them diminutive so a herd of 
cows can fit in a pocket. 
Skele–Gro 
Difficulty: 12
A dreadful tasting potion 
that mends broken 
bones and can even 
regrow entire bones that 
have vanished or been 
lost in some way. The 
regrowing of bones is a 
painful and slow 
process. Standard Skele–
Grow can take more than a 
full day to regrow bones. 
Highly effective Skele–Gro 
can do be same job in three or 
four hours. 
Sleeping Draught 
Difficulty: 15
A purple potion that sends the 
drinker into a deep, dreamless 
sleep. A more gentle. Version 
of the Draught of Living 
Death. Larger creatures like 
Dragons and Trolls may 
require a highly effective 
draught to be put to sleep. 
Strengthening 
Solution 
Difficulty: 12
Grants the drinker immense 
strength for up to half an hour. 
A normal dose adds +5 to your 
Vitality attribute. A highly 
effective dose add +10. 
Swelling Solution 
Difficulty: 9
Causes whatever it touches to 
swell in size like an 
Engorgement Charm. The 
effect lasts for two to three 
hours or until a Deflating 
Draught is taken. A highly 
effective Swelling Draught can 
last up to 24 hours or until a 
highly effective Deflating 
Draught is administered. 
Veritaserum 
Difficulty: 18
Veritaserum is a powerful 
truth serum. The potion forces 
the drinker to answer any 
questions put to them 
truthfully, though there are 
certain methods of resistance 
like the Veritaserum Antidote 
and Occlumency skill (see 
page 23). In addition, a Secret 
Keeper under a Fidelius 
Charm (see page 51) cannot be 
forced to reveal their secret 
using Veritaserum. When 
using Occlumency (1D+Focus 
+Occlumency) the target to 
roll to lie in answer to a 
question is 12+ for a standard 
dose or 15+ for a highly 
effective dose. In theory, 
use of Veritaserum is strictly 
controlled by the British 
Ministry of Magic 
Veritaserum 
Antidote 
Difficulty: 18
Antidote to the truth serum 
Veritaserum. A highly effective 
VeritaserumAntidote is 
required to combat a highly 
effective Veritaserum. 
Wit–sharpening 
Potion 
Difficulty: 12
Allows the drinker to think 
more clearly for up to one 
hour. A normal dose adds +3
to your Wits attribute. A highly 
effective dose add +5. 
Wolfsbane Potion 
Difficulty: 18
Relieves, but does not cure, 
the symptoms of Lycanthropy. 
A goblet full must be taken 
each day during the week 
leading to the full moon. 
Missing even one dosage will 
make it ineffective. The potion 
allows the Lycanthrope (or 
Werewolf) to retain their 
mental faculties while 
transformed. A highly effective 
Wolfsbane Potion may even 
allow a strong minded 
Lycanthrope to retain their 
human form by rolling 
1D+Focus+Occlumency 
with a target of 15+. 
Wound–cleaning 
Potion 
Difficulty: 15
A powerful antiseptic in the 
form of a purple potion that 
smokes and stings when 
applied. A normal dose 
removes any chance of 
infection and speeds healing 
tenfold. In addition, a highly 
effective dose can also ward of 
magical infections like 
Lycanthropy if the wounds are 
treated in time.
