Animal Companions
At Hogwarts, students are 
permitted to bring a pet (see 
page 12); an owl, a cat, or a 
toad. The rule is practical as 
much as sentimental. Owls 
carry messages, cats keep 
down pests, and toads are 
manageable in crowded 
dormitories. 
These animal companions are 
much loved by their owners. 
Harry’s Snowy Owl, Hedwig, is 
his messenger and a constant 
presence through Harry’s 
early years. Hermione’s Half–
Kneazle Cat, Crookshanks, 
proves unusually perceptive, 
recognising threats that others 
overlook. Ron’s Rat, Scabbers, 
appears ordinary at first, a 
flea–bitten hand–me–down 
pet, but his true identity turns 
out to be far more sinister. 
Beyond the standard list of 
permitted animals are more 
singular creatures. Fawkes the 
Phoenix, loyal to Albus 
Dumbledore, demonstrates 
rare magical power and 
unwavering allegiance. 
In darker contrast, slithers 
Nagini, bound to Lord 
Voldemort, whose role as a 
Horcrux (see page @@) makes 
her far more than just a 
faithful companion. 
These animals influence key 
events during the Harry Potter 
books and add an extra 
dimension to the characters 
they stand beside. Your 
characters’ pets, under the 
control of the Narrator, may 
also surprise you in ways that 
advance your story and drag 
you off in unexpected 
directions. 
Nagini 
Species: Snake 
Owner: Voldemort 
Vitality 6
Speed 7 (Strike 12) 
Wits 9
Knowledge – 
Focus – 
Move: 2D+3 (2D+8 strike) 
Combat: 2D+5 (2D toxic 
venom*, see page 38) 
Fear: 8
Description 
Nagini was originally human, 
but cursed to transform into a 
Snake. Eventually, she took 
permanent Snake form and 
lost her human mind. She 
came into the possession of 
Lord Voldemort, who made 
her into one of his Horcruxes 
(see page 129). Nagini was 
killed at the end of the 
Second Wizarding War. 
As the last Horcrux to be 
destroyed, her death left 
Lord Voldemort vulnerable. 
* Nagini’s venom requires a 
specialist antidote. It delays 
blood clotting and dissolves 
stitches, which causes the 
wound to bleed profusely.

understanding Harry’s wishes

Scabbers

Owner: Ron Weasley 
Vitality 5
Speed 10
Wits 8
Knowledge 2
Focus 1
Move: 2D+5
Combat: 2D+3 (1D+1 Bite) 
Fear: 2
Skills:

Description
Scabbers was an unusually old 
and scraggly–looking rat that 
was passed down to Ron by 
his brother, Percy. When 
Sirius Black and Remus 
Lupin, members of the Order 
of the Phoenix (see page125), 
revealed that Scabbers was 
actually an Animagus named 
Peter Pettigrew (see page 130), 
Ron refused to believe it until 
they forced the rat to 
transform back into his 
human form. Ron took this 
revelation as a personal insult, 
and refused to listen to 
Pettigrew's pleas.

Wits 7

Description
Hermione Granger's pet cat, 
Crookshanks, is half–Kneazle, 
as evidenced by his lion–like 
appearance, ability to solve 
problems on his own, and 
ability to recognise 
untrustworthy individuals, 
even if they are in their 
Animagus form. 
Sirius Black (see page 125) 
claimed that Crookshanks was 
the most intelligent cat he had 
ever met. Crookshanks, 
although frequently bad 
tempered, is very playful and 
enjoys catching spiders and 
chasing gnomes.

Description
Fawkes is a highly intelligent 
male phoenix and Albus 
Dumbledore's animal 
companion and defender. 
Fawkes had been in 
Dumbledore's service for 59
years up to the Headmaster's 
death in 1997. His tears, like 
the tears of all phoenixes, 
possess healing properties and 
saved Harry's life after his arm 
was punctured by the 
basilisk's venomous fang.
