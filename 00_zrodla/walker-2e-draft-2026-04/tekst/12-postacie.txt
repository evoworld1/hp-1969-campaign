Characters
The wizarding world that 
Harry Potter lives in is 
full of fascinating and 
eccentric characters. Many are 
friendly, some are more 
ambiguous, and a notable few 
are decidedly hostile. This 
section gives stats for many 
of the significant characters 
encountered in the books. 
Harry and his close friends, 
Ron and Hermione, are 
covered in great detail with 
stats outlining their abilities 
at the ages of 11, 14, and 17, 
which represent important 
stages in their development. 
Other Hogwarts students are 
described as they appear in 
the later books at the point 
where they begin to join Harry 
in his fight against the Dark 
Lord. You can easily shave off 
a few points here and there if 
you want players to meet them 
earlier. Stats are also given for 
many older wizards, including 
the Weasley family, Hogwarts 
staff, members of the Order of 
the Phoenix, and of course 
Lord Voldemort and his death 
eaters. Where characters have 
special skills they are marked 
with an asterisk (*). 
Notable Spells 
The wizards featured here are 
highly accomplished in the 
magical arts (with a few 
exceptions) and only a few 
notable spells that they are 
known to have used to great 
effect are listed, rather than a 
laborious list for each 
character. A Charms Master 
like Professor Flitwick will 
know every charm going, but 
there are certain spells that he 
has famously used and may 
even cast with greater than the 
usual effect. As well as those 
spells listed, nearly all adult 
wizards will be able to 
apparate (instantly move from 
place to place via the 
Apparition spell) and be able 
to cast most spells taught 
during the first couple of years 
at Hogwarts (see pageS 15–16). 
In addition, the majority of the 
witches and wizards listed 
here have successfully learned 
to cast the powerful protective 
spell Expecto Patronum (see 
page 51). Where they have 
managed to produce the more 
powerful corporeal Patronus, 
the animal form of their 
Patronus is noted. A non–
corporeal Patronus is 
indicated by the letters NC. 
Where different versions of a 
character are given, their older 
versions will of course still be 
able to cast the spells listed 
under their younger selves as 
well as any others that seem 
appropriate.

Character 
We’ve included as many 
characters as possible to 
reflect the world of Harry 
Potter, but there are many 
we’ve left out for reasons of 
space. You can easily make 
your own versions of 
characters in the 
books using the 
following tables 
as a guide.

Harry Potter 
Harry James Potter (born 31st 
July 1980) is an English half￾blood wizard, and one of the 
most famous wizards of 
modern times. His parents, 
Lily and James Potter, fought 
against the dark wizard Lord 
Voldemort during the First 
Wizarding War. Harry's birth 
was overshadowed by a 
prophecy naming him as the 
one with the power to defeat 
the dark Lord. When Harry 
was a year and three months 
old, Voldemort set out to 
frustrate the prophecy, 
murdering Harry's parents as 
they tried to protect him. 
Lily Potter’s love for her son 
created an impenetrable 
barrier, deflecting Voldemort’s 
deadly curse and seemingly 
destroying him. The First 
Wizarding War was at an end. 
Harry became known as the 
Boy Who Lived. The only 
known survivor of the Killing 
Curse. 
Harry was given over to the 
care of his only remaining 
relatives, the Dursley’s. 
Muggles who treated Harry 
more like a servant than a 
relative because of his Aunt 
Petunia’s jealousy towards her 
witch sister. The Dursleys hid 
his wizarding ancestry from 
him and made him sleep in 
the cupboard under the stairs. 
On his eleventh birthday, 
Harry learned that he was a 
wizard, and (despite the 
Dursley’s objections) began 
attending Hogwarts School of 
Witchcraft and Wizardry 
where the young wizard was 
sorted into Gryffindor House. 
Harry became best friends 
with fellow Gryffindor 
students, Ron Weasley and 
Hermione Granger. 
In his first year, Harry became 
the youngest Quidditch 
Seeker in over a century and 
stopped Voldemort from 
getting his clutches on the 
Philosophers Stone which 
would have restored the Dark 
Lord to corporeal form. In the 
second year he solved the 
mystery of the Chamber of 
Secrets and discovered that he 
could somehow speak 
Parseltongue, the language of 
serpents and a gift normally 
associated with dark wizards. 
Harry would later realise that 
a fragment of Voldemort’s 
soul had lodged in him during 
the failed attempt to murder 
him as an infant. In his third 
year he found that he had an 
uncle, Sirius Black, who he 
initially believed to have 
betrayed his parents but 
instead turned out to be on 
the trail of the real traitor. 
Black had escaped from 
Azkaban, the wizarding prison, 
pursued by Dementors. 
Azkaban’s Phantom guards 
who fed on misery. Remus 
Lupin, a childhood friend of 
his parents and briefly a 
Professor at the school, taught 
Harry the challenging 
Patronus Charm to protect 
him from the Dementors who 
craved the sustenance 
afforded by the tragedy in 
Harry’s life. 
In his fourth year, Harry won 
the Triwizard Tournament and 
witnessed Lord Voldemort’s 
rebirth. During the next year, 
Harry reluctantly taught and 
led Dumbledore's Army. 
Fighting a guerrilla war 
against the Ministry of Magic, 
now secretly under 
Voldemort’s control. 
Harry played a significant role 
in many battles of the Second 
Wizarding War between 1997
and 1998. Hunting down and 
destroying Horcruxes. Shards 
of Voldemort’s shattered soul. 
While they existed, Voldemort 
could never be truly slain. 
Finally Harry confronted 
Voldemort and nearly 
sacrificed himself, knowing 
that doing so was the only way 
to destroy the fragment of 
Voldemort's soul within him. 
In limbo, Harry had to choose 
between living or moving on. 
Harry chose to live. After he 
awoke, Harry faced Voldemort 
for the final time and defeated 
him once and for all. 
Harry possessed a number of 
powerful artefacts. Including 
an invisibility cloak that had 
belonged to his father, and the 
Marauder’s Map. A magical 
map of Hogwarts that showed 
all the secret passages and 
tracked the movement of 
everybody in the castle.

Hermione Granger 
Hermione Jean Granger (born 
19th September 1979) is an 
English Muggle-born witch 
born to Mr and Mrs Granger. 
Married dentists living on the 
outskirts of London. At the 
age of eleven, Hermione and 
her family learned about her 
magical nature and the 
wizarding world she was 
invited to enter. Hermione 
was accepted into Hogwarts 
School of Witchcraft and 
Wizardry and began attending 
in 1991 where she was Sorted 
into Gryffindor House. 
Hermione possessed an 
unusually brilliant academic 
mind and proved to be a gifted 
student in almost every 
subject that she studied. 
Despite some missteps along 
the way, she became firm 
friends with Harry Potter and 
Ron Weasley. 
In Hermione’s first year, she 
was almost killed by a 
Mountain Troll. Her rescue by 
Harry and Ron cemented their 
friendship. She later returned 
the favour by solving two of 
the four puzzles required to 
reach the Philosophers Stone. 
In her second year, Hermione 
had a key role in the discovery 
of the Chamber of Secrets, 
before falling victim to the 
basilisk that had been released 
from the hidden Chamber. 
However, she recovered from 
the petrification under the 
care of Madam Pomfrey with 
Professor Sprout's Mandrake 
Restorative Draught. 
The next year, Hermione was 
granted permission to use a 
Time-Turner from the 
Ministry of Magic to make it 
possible for her to study far 
more subjects than were 
possible without time travel. 
Hermione and Harry later 
used it to rescue Harry’s uncle, 
Sirius Black, from the 
Dementors. 
During her fourth year, 
Hermione became an advocate 
for the better treatment of 
House–Elves and helped in 
Harry's preparation for the 
Triwizard Tournament. In her 
fifth year, Hermione was the 
driving force behind the 
creation of Dumbledore's 
Army. 
Hermione played a significant 
role in many battles of the 
Second Wizarding War and 
worked with Ron to aid Harry 
in his quest to find and 
destroy Lord Voldemort's 
Horcruxes (hidden fragments 
of his soul) to render the Dark 
Lord Mortal. Hermione then 
played a pivotal part in the 
Battle of Hogwarts which 
ended in Voldemort’s defeat 
and final death.

Ron Weasley 
Ronald Bilius ‘Ron’ Weasley 
(born 1st March 1980) is an 
English pure-blood wizard, 
the sixth and youngest son of 
Arthur and Molly Weasley. 
Ron had five older brothers 
(in order of seniority); Bill, 
Charlie, Percy, and Fred and 
George (twins). Ron lived in 
the shadow of his older 
brothers’ accomplishments. 
Ron also has a younger sister 
called Ginny. 
Ron and his siblings lived at 
the The Burrow, on the 
outskirts of Ottery St 
Catchpole, Devon. 
Ron began attending 
Hogwarts School of Witchcraft 
and Wizardry in 1991 and was 
Sorted into Gryffindor House. 
He soon became close friends 
with fellow student Harry 
Potter and later, Hermione 
Granger. 
In the first year at Hogwarts, 
Ron helped Harry protect the 
Philosophers Stone by 
winning a deadly game of 
Wizards Chess played with 
gigantic pieces. 
At the beginning of the 
second year, Ron ‘borrowed’ 
his father’s enchanted Ford 
Anglia to fly himself and 
Harry to Hogwarts. A decision 
that left him in considerable 
hot water. 
In his third year he was 
startled to discover that his pet 
rat was in fact the betrayer of 
Harry’s parents, the Animagus 
Peter Pettigrew. 
In the fourth year, Ron fell out 
with Harry over Harry being 
chosen to compete in the 
Triwizard Tournament. After 
seeing the dangers that Harry 
faced, Ron soon saw the error 
of his ways. 
In the fifth year, Ron was 
among the first to join 
Dumbledore’s Army. 
Alongside training in defence 
against the dark arts, Ron also 
became a prefect and Seeker 
on the Gryffindor Quidditch 
team. 
During his sixth year, Ron 
almost died after drinking 
poisoned Mead intended for 
Dumbledore and was 
enchanted by mistakenly 
eating love potion infused 
chocolates intended for Harry. 
In many ways it was not a 
happy year for Ron. 
Ron joined Harry and 
Hermione in not returning for 
their seventh year and instead 
searched for Voldemort’s 
Horcruxes. Ron would go on 
to fight alongside his friends 
at the battle of Hogwarts.

Slytherin Students 
Slytherin House has a well–
earned reputation for creating 
dark witches and wizards. 
Voldemort himself was a 
Slytherin when he attended 
Hogwarts, and most Slytherins 
share his hatred for Muggles 
and the Muggle born. Not all 
Slytherin are pureblood 
(Voldemort himself is Half–
blood), but the majority of 
Slytherins come from Pure–
blood families where blood 
purity prejudice is the norm. 
Slytherin and Gryffindor are 
arch rivals at Quidditch and in 
vying for the House Cup, and 
no love is lost between the two 
houses. There was a 
particularly strong enmity 
between Harry Potter and the 
Slytherin student Draco 
Malfoy (backed up by his de 
facto bodyguards, Crabbe and 
Goyle). Draco is the son of 
Lucius and Narcissa Malfoy, 
and is expected to follow their 
path as a Deatheater serving 
Lord Voldemort. 
Slytherin students are the 
natural bad guys of any Harry 
Potter story, but there is no 
reason that players couldn’t 
be Slytherins with the 
Narrator’s approval. If you 
want to portray Slytherin 
students, it is important not to 
think of them as outright 
villains. They display many 
positive traits including 
cleverness, resourcefulness, 
and determination. Even 
Draco came to realise in the 
end that his path did not lie 
with the Deatheaters.
